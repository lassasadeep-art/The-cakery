Need Help?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you order your special cake with total peace of mind.

How far in advance should I place my order?

For standard signature cakes, we appreciate 24 to 48 hours notice. For elaborate custom fondant, themed sculpts, or tiered wedding cakes, please reach out at least 3 to 5 days ahead to ensure slot availability.

Do you deliver across Colombo and suburbs?

Yes! We provide safe, air-conditioned vehicle delivery across Colombo 1-15, Rajagiriya, Battaramulla, Nugegoda, Dehiwala, Mount Lavinia, Thalawathugoda, Kotte, and surrounding suburbs. Pickup is also available by arrangement.

Can I customize colors, cake toppers, and messages?

Absolutely! You can choose custom color palettes, edible gold flakes, personalized acrylic or wooden cake toppers, custom inscriptions, and special flavor combinations. Just send your reference photo via WhatsApp!

Why join the WhatsApp Group?

Our WhatsApp group members get weekly exclusive discount codes, flash announcements for extra fresh bakes, priority booking during busy holiday weekends, and direct 1-on-1 consultations with our pastry chef.

How should I store and serve the cake?

Keep the cake refrigerated until 30-45 minutes before slicing. Serving at room temperature allows the pure dairy butter and chocolate ganache to soften into maximum silky, rich perfection.
